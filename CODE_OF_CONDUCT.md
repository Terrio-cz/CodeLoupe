# Code of Conduct

This is a short code of conduct for the CodeLoupe repository, its issues, pull requests and wiki. It follows the spirit of
the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), which is
licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to the topic.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; criticise the code or the idea, not the person.
- Assume good faith, and say so when you do not understand rather than when you disagree.
- Stay on topic and keep private information, yours or anybody else's, out of the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Insults, derogatory comments, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Harassment of any kind, in public or in private, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing somebody else's private information, such as an address or an e-mail address, without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

The maintainers (the administrators of the repository) are responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove,
edit or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a person for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful. They
will explain the reason where it is safe to do so.

## Reporting

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment or issue (it reaches the GitHub staff and the
organisation), or open an issue titled "Conduct report" that contains no details, only that you want to report something
privately, and a maintainer will arrange a private channel with you. Reports are treated confidentially, and the
maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of the person who reports.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone represents the project in public. It is adapted from the
Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
